    How to take a screenshot instead
    • Chromebook: press Ctrl + Show windows (the key with boxes). For part of the screen: Ctrl + Shift + Show windows.
    • Windows: press Windows key + Shift + S, then drag over the report.
    • Mac: press Command + Shift + 4, then drag over the report.
    • iPad: press the top button and a volume button at the same time.
    • iPhone: press the side button and volume up at the same time (older iPhones: side and Home).
    • Android phone: press power and volume down at the same time.
