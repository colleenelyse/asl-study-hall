How do I log into my school Google account?

Your class app only works with your school Google account (the email your school gave you), not a personal one. Pick the device you are using.

ComputerSchool ChromebookPhone or iPadStill stuck?

💻 On a computer (Chrome)

  1. Look at the top right corner of Chrome. Click your picture or letter in the circle. You see a list of the Google accounts on this computer.
  2. Is your school email in the list? Click it. A new Chrome window opens, signed in with your school account.
  3. Not in the list? Click Add (or Add another account), then Sign in. Type your school email, click Next, type your school password, and click Next.
  4. In that window, open your class app link again (from Google Classroom or your teacher). The app opens and shows your name.

🏫 On a school Chromebook

  1. School Chromebooks are already signed in with your school account. Open the class app link from Google Classroom on the Chromebook.
  2. Still asked to sign in? Click the time in the bottom right corner, click Sign out, and sign back in with your school email and password.

📱 On a phone or iPad

  1. Open the link in the Chrome app (or Safari).
  2. If Google asks you to choose an account, tap your school email. If it isn't there, tap Use another account and sign in with your school email and password.
  3. Already signed in with a personal account? In Chrome, tap your picture at the top right, then tap your school account (or Add account). Open the link again.

💡 A quick fix that often works

Open a private window: press Ctrl + Shift + N (on a Mac: ⌘ + Shift + N). Paste the class app link there and sign in with only your school account.

🙋 Still stuck?

Ask your teacher for a login code. On the app's first screen, tap the login code button and type it in.

Forgot your school password? Ask your school's tech team. Your teacher can't see or change it.
