Letters

Ages

Every age starts at the chin. 1–9: the number, palm out, pushed down and forward. 10 and up: the 1 on the chin, then the number.

Years

Choose the kinds of years. Teen years split in two (17 + 76); 2000–2009 slide the 0; 2010–2099 are TWENTY + the last two.

Numbers

Green numbers are in the game; white ones are not. Tap a number to add it or take it out.
