Book, check or cancel your Testing Center appointments.
Open the Testing Center

Sign in with your school Google account.

Put it on your home screen

Save it like an app, with the LT lion as its icon. Then it’s one tap away whenever you need to book, check or cancel.

📱 iPhone or iPad (Safari)

  1. Open this page in Safari: asl.cool/testingcenter/app
  2. Tap the Share button ↑︎ (the square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

📱 Android phone (Chrome)

  1. Open this page in Chrome: asl.cool/testingcenter/app
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app), then Add.

💻 Chromebook (Chrome)

  1. Open this page: asl.cool/testingcenter/app
  2. Click the ⋮ menu, then Cast, save, and share › Create shortcut…
  3. Click Create. The lion shows up in your launcher; right-click it there to pin it to your shelf.

The icon opens the real Testing Center app, the same one as the link from your teacher. Your information stays in the school’s Google account. How to book
