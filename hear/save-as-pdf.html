Save your score as a PDF

Font:

Your score report can be saved as a PDF file. A PDF is a file you can upload to an online assignment, such as one in Canvas.

Before you start

  1. Type your name in the Your name box next to the score.
  2. Press Score report. Check that the report shows your name and your score.
  3. Leave the report open. Do not reload or close the page: the score is only kept while the page is open.

Part 1: Save the report as a PDF

Press the Print button on the report. A print window opens. You are not going to print on paper. You are going to choose "Save as PDF" where the printer would normally be picked. Find your browser below.

  1. In the print window, find the line called Destination.
  2. Click the menu beside it and choose Save as PDF. If you do not see it, choose See more… first.
  3. Click the blue Save button.
  4. Give the file a name with your own name in it, such as Ear Quiz - Jordan Lee.
  5. Choose where to save it. Downloads is a good choice because it is easy to find again. On a Chromebook you can also choose Google Drive.
  6. Click Save.

Part 2: Check the file

  1. Open the place where you saved it (for example, your Downloads folder).
  2. Open the PDF. Make sure it shows your name, the date and your score.

Part 3: Upload it to your assignment

  1. Open the assignment your teacher gave you.
  2. Press the button that starts your submission. In Canvas this is Start Assignment.
  3. Choose to upload a file. In Canvas, choose File Upload, then Upload File or Choose File.
  4. Find the PDF you saved and select it.
  5. Press Submit Assignment.
  6. Check that the assignment page now says it was submitted.

Other sites, such as Google Classroom, work in a similar way: open the assignment, add or attach a file, choose your PDF, and turn it in. If your screen looks different, ask your teacher.

If something goes wrong

ProblemWhat to try
The PDF shows the whole page, not just the report.
  1. Close the print window.
  2. Open the Score report first, then press the Print button that is on the report itself.
The PDF says "(no name typed)".
  1. Close the report.
  2. Type your name in the Your name box, open the report again, and save a new PDF.
I cannot find the file I saved.
  1. Look in your Downloads folder.
  2. If it is not there, save it again and read the folder name before you click Save.
My score went back to zero.
  1. The page was reloaded or closed. The score is only kept while the page is open.
  2. Save the PDF before you leave the page next time.
