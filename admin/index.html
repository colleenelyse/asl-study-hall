2. The buttons

Emoji, name, a short description, and the address (for a page on asl.cool, just the part after asl.cool/, like fingerspelling/; for another site, the whole address starting with https://).

You always see every button after typing your own password. Choose who sees each button in the Passwords and Teacher Links boxes below (the Opens: ticks).

Passwords (for classes, teachers or anyone)

Make a password for anyone: a class, a class period, another teacher, a family. Whoever types it on asl.cool sees only the buttons you tick for that password (the Opens: ticks in its box). Each must be different, and different from your own password. Remove a password and it stops working right away. You type your own password in the same box to see everything.

Teacher Links

Each link has a random code nobody can guess, and shows only the buttons you tick for it. Remove a link and it stops working right away. The names are only for you; teachers never see them.

🛍️ Shop Links on asl.cool

Only for people who come to asl.cool without a password. Anyone who types a password, or opens a teacher link, gets that password's or link's own 🛍️ Show the shop tick instead, and keeps it on that device (so students stay shop-free even on the fingerspelling site). Ticked: visitors see the Shop ASL Study Hall and TpT buttons, the product cards and the teacher card. Unticked: hidden. Never shown inside ASL Study or your TpT products. Click Save at the bottom after changing it.

Featured Products (shown on asl.cool)

Product cards visitors see on asl.cool (at the bottom of the welcome page, and at the end of a round on the public fingerspelling site; never inside ASL Study or your TpT products). For each: a name, the link (your Shopify or TpT product page), and a picture address (in Shopify or TpT, right-click the product picture › Copy Image Address). Price, a short description and a label (like "On TpT" or "New") are optional. Leave this empty to show every product in your Shopify store automatically (new products appear by themselves). Add cards here only to choose specific ones or to add TpT listings. While Show the shop to visitors is off, these are hidden too.
