❄️ Frozen: students see this picture

📽️ Projector

This window goes on the projector screen and shows a live copy of your main monitor. Press ❄️ Freeze to hold the picture still while you use your monitor. Timers and the bell schedule on top keep running.

  1. Drag this window onto the projector screen.
  2. Press Start. Choose Entire screen, pick your main monitor (not the projector), then press Share.
  3. Press ⛶ Full screen in the bar at the bottom. The bar appears whenever you move the mouse over this screen.

Your projector has to be set to Extended display, not Mirror. On a Mac: System Settings → Displays → click the projector → Use as: Extended display. Shortcut keys on this screen: F freezes and unfreezes.

Unfreezing from your own monitor: use the 📽️ Freeze button in the teacher view's top bar, or press 🎛️ Mini control in the bar here for a small Freeze button that floats above every window (drag it onto your private monitor).
