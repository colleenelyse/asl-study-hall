Where student data lives

All student information stays in a Google Sheet in the school's Google account

Students, schedules, appointments, teacher instructions and the activity log are rows in one Google Sheet owned by a school Google account in the district's Google Workspace. The app itself is Google Apps Script attached to that same sheet.

  • No outside database, vendor or subscription: nothing is copied out of the school's Google account.
  • The sheet is shared only with Testing Center staff; students and teachers never open it. They see only what the app shows them, after signing in with their school account.
  • The district keeps control: it owns the account, and Google Workspace's own security, backups and admin controls apply.
  • Imports from Infinite Campus go straight into a tab of the same sheet.

Students

Booking takes a minute, and the rules are built in

Students open the app with their school account and land on their own Testing Center page.

My Testing Center

  • Upcoming appointments, waitlist places and offers, with Cancel one tap away.
  • My info and My classes straight from Infinite Campus (name with nickname, ID, grade, campus, periods, teachers, rooms).
  • If their schedule changed, they can fix it or paste it from Infinite Campus; staff see that it was student-entered.
Try it as a student →

Pick a class, a test, a time

  • Their real classes in one dropdown: no typing teacher names.
  • Only open periods, with seats left. Closed periods (like North's Monday 6th) are enforced, and a period stays open until it starts by that day's bell schedule.
  • Lunch/study hall periods can be booked as the A half, the B half or the whole period, and seats are counted for each half.
  • Full? Join the waitlist. An open seat is offered by email for a short time; the student signs in to accept.

Missing a class? It says so

  • If the period has an academic class, students see the Testing Center's rule before booking.
  • "My teacher gave me permission" is recorded with a note, and staff are reminded to check the written pass.
  • Study hall, lunch and support periods never warn.

Teachers

One form per test, instead of a paper sheet per student

Teachers open the app and see their own students' Testing Center appointments.

My classes

  • Every upcoming appointment, marked Ready, Check or Needs info.
  • Standing resources: a live doc of access codes, a Drive folder or a Canvas link, attached once to a course or to all their classes. Students never see them.
  • "My usual formats": paper or online per course, so the center can prepare early.
Try it as a teacher →

The digital testing form

  • The paper Make-Up Testing Procedure Form, online: format, platform, access code, time limit, calculators, notes.
  • Filled in once per test, it goes to every student taking that test.
  • Opened straight from the Testing Center's email reminder, signed in, for that teacher's class only.

Student view

  • Teachers can look at any of their own students' pages exactly as the student sees them.
  • Look only: nothing can be booked or canceled for the student.

Testing Center staff

The coordinator hub: the whole day on one screen

Password-protected, for the Testing Center coordinators. Each campus is separate; staff can be given view or edit access to the other one.

Today

  • Period by period: who's coming, A/B half, paper or online, Ready or Needs info, and seats used.
  • Check in → start → done, with times recorded. "Done" says exactly what's known (student-reported, staff-confirmed, submission verified, paper collected).
  • Bell.plus is always open in the corner. Walk-ins, extra seats or a closed period for one day, and a list of students for Securly.

Numbered desks

  • The room is drawn once with a seating-chart tool; desks are numbered like restaurant tables.
  • At check-in, the app suggests the free desk farthest from other testers; tap it or any free desk.
  • The Room tab shows who is at each desk and for how long.

Needs attention

  • Missing paper tests or access codes, passes to check, seats waiting for a waitlist decision, emails that didn't send, students whose schedules changed.
  • Most urgent first, each with the button to fix it.

Teacher emails, under control

  • Missing-materials reminders the school day before (using the real school calendar), as drafts to review or sent automatically, your choice.
  • One email per test even with co-teachers or several students; nothing is repeated, nothing is sent to a guessed address.
  • Optional "completed" notes to teachers.

A fair waitlist

  • Kept in the order students joined. An open seat is held for the next student for a set time; if they decline or don't answer, it moves on.
  • Every offer, acceptance and expiration is logged.

Infinite Campus, once a week (or whenever)

  • The Infinite Campus schedule export goes into one tab of the sheet; the app reads all 94,000 rows on Google's side, with a progress bar.
  • A preview with real counts (new students, schedule changes, withdrawals) before anything changes; one click applies it, all or nothing, with a backup.
  • Clubs and activities are left out; nobody is withdrawn unless staff confirm the export is complete.

When the roster is behind

  • Staff can add a class or a teacher for a student on the spot; it's marked "added by the Testing Center" until the next Infinite Campus export.

Privacy and safety

Designed for a district review

Where the data lives

  • One Google Sheet in a school Google account in the district's Google Workspace, shared only with Testing Center staff. No outside services or databases.
  • The app is open only to school Google accounts; the server checks every request (students see only their own records; teachers only their own students).
  • Access codes and test links never reach students or student emails.
  • No IEP or 504 details are stored; the form has an extended-time line only.

Safeguards

  • The coordinator hub needs a password (stored scrambled) on top of school sign-in; it locks after 6 hours.
  • Every sensitive change is logged (who, what, when).
  • Roster imports are previewed, backed up and reversible; a file that looks incomplete is held.
  • History is kept: appointments are never rewritten when schedules change.
