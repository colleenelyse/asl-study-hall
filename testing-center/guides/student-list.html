Upload a new student list

Until IT approves an automatic connection, the student list is updated by hand: an Infinite Campus export pasted into the Testing Center sheet, then checked and applied in the app. It takes about 15 minutes, most of it waiting. Do it at the start of each semester, and any week schedules change a lot.

1. Export from Infinite Campus

  1. Run the same schedule report as last time (every student, every class, all 27 columns, starting with student.studentNumber).
  2. Include both campuses (or one campus at a time) and every grade.
  3. Save it as CSV. Don't open it in Excel first; Excel drops the zeros at the start of student numbers.

2. Put it in the sheet

  1. Open the Testing Center sheet and click the IC_Raw_Import tab at the bottom.
  2. File › Import › Upload, and choose the CSV.
  3. Import location: Replace current sheet (not "Replace spreadsheet").
  4. Uncheck Convert text to numbers, dates, and formulas.
  5. Click Import data. You should see the export's rows, with headers like student.studentNumber in row 1.

3. Process it in the app

  1. Open the Testing Center app and press 🔒 Testing Center Coordinator Log In.
  2. Go to Settings › Student list & imports.
  3. Click Process Infinite Campus Import.
  4. A progress bar shows Validating, then Reading, then Matching. A whole-school export takes a few minutes. You can leave the page; it keeps going and picks up where it left off.

4. Check the numbers

  • When it says Ready for review, a summary opens: students, classes, new enrollments, and what will change.
  • Clubs and activities (codes starting with Z), rows with no room, and odd period ends are skipped automatically. They aren't classes.
  • Want to see names? Click Inspect the changes.
  • Numbers look wrong (far too few students)? Click Cancel import. Nothing has changed yet.

5. Apply

  1. If the export was everyone on that campus, tick This export is the complete schedule. Students who left and classes they dropped are then ended (kept as history, not deleted). If it was only part of the school, leave it unticked: it only adds and updates.
  2. Leave Empty the IC_Raw_Import tab afterward ticked. The app keeps what it needs, and the sheet stays small.
  3. Click Apply changes. You should see Infinite Campus import applied, and last synced shows today.

6. Tidy up (two minutes)

  1. Go to Settings › Tidy up.
  2. Fill in any new email addresses. For teachers with two last names, pick which one the school used.
  3. Click Clean up what's ticked.

If something goes wrong

  • Undo: Settings › Student list & imports › Import history › Undo puts the student and class tabs back the way they were. The last two imports can be undone.
  • "Missing columns": the wrong report was exported. Run the schedule report with all 27 columns.
  • Stuck on Paused: press Continue.

What never changes: appointments, notes, teachers' testing forms and access-code links stay as they are. Students who left keep their history. All student data stays in the Testing Center Google Sheet in the school's Google account.
