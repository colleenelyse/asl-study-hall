Running the day: the Coordinator hub

Sign in with your school Google account, press 🔒 Testing Center Coordinator Log In at the top right, and enter the coordinator password (staff on the no-password list go straight in). The app opens on the teacher dashboard; ← Teacher view takes you back. Step away? Tap Lock the hub. Bell.plus is always in the corner; tap ▾ to make it small.

Today: period by period

  1. Check in: scan or type a student ID in the check-in box at the top, or tap Arrived. Pick a desk on the room map; the clock starts.
  2. Check out: tap Check out to end the test and save what’s known. Add a note for the teacher if something happened.
  3. No-shows: tap Mark no-shows for a period that has ended (it also happens on its own at 3:30).
  4. Tap Room to see the desk map. Rows with missing test info have a red edge.

Needs attention

The list beside Today. ⏰ Right now means a test is this period or next and the center doesn’t have it. Choose Cancel & email student, Email the teacher or Where's the teacher?.

Schedule

  • Open periods: tap a period to close it, leave early, close the whole day, or reopen. Booked students can be canceled with an emailed reason.
  • Waitlist: who is waiting, and for when.

People

  • Search students and teachers; see teacher resources.
  • Walk-ins: Book for this student. Roster out of date? Use + Add a class… or + Add a teacher….

Emails

  • Missing-materials reminders: preview, then send.
  • The email log shows everything that went out.

Settings

Switches for seats, booking on/off, emails, the Bell.plus link, the no-password list and the hub password. Also: the daily health check, the Infinite Campus import, the room drawing, and reports.

Rule of thumb: red first. Clear ⏰ Right now alerts, then red rows, then the rest of Needs attention.
