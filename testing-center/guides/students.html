Booking the Testing Center

Need to make up a test, retake one, or finish one you started? Sign in with your school Google account and the app opens My Testing Center. You only ever see your own appointments.

1. Pick a time

  1. Tap a day tab at the top.
  2. You only see times that are still open that day.
  3. Tap a period. For periods 4 and 5 (lunch / study hall) choose A only, B only or Whole period.

2. Tell us about the test

  1. Choose the class from your class list (or My class isn't listed).
  2. Type or pick the test.
  3. Choose Makeup test, Retake or Finish a test I started.
  4. Choose online or paper, then book it.

3. Missing a class to test?

  1. If the period is during an academic class, you’ll see a warning.
  2. Only if that teacher said yes, tap ✋ (your teacher) gave me permission, and I'll have a written note when I arrive.
  3. Bring the written note with you.

4. Period full? Join the waitlist

  1. Tap Join waitlist.
  2. If a seat opens, you get an email.
  3. Sign in and tap Accept before the offer runs out. The email alone does not book you.

5. After you book

  • Tap Add to my Google Calendar.
  • You can book until the period starts.
  • Bring your student ID to the Testing Center.

6. Can’t make it? Cancel

  1. Tap Cancel, then Yes, cancel it when it asks “Are you sure you want to cancel?”
  2. Reminder emails have a cancel link too.
  3. Missed one? At 3:30 you get an email: Save it, I'll make another appointment or Toss it.

Your info

My info and My classes show what the school has on file. Something wrong? Tap Fix my schedule. Want different colors? Use the 🎨 Colors menu at the top.

Your information stays in a Google Sheet in the school’s Google account. Your teachers see only their own students’ appointments.
