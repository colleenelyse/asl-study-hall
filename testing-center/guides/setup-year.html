Set up your Testing Center year

About ten minutes once a year, and the Testing Center runs most of the year without asking you: it has your codes, your rules and your usual formats for every student who tests. The first time you open the app after August 1, a 🗓️ Set up my year window walks you through it, with last year’s answers filled in.

Before you start: your access-code doc

  1. In the app, open My classes and click Make my copy of the template (or keep last year’s doc).
  2. Name it Access Codes – your last name.
  3. For each class, fill in the table: Test · Access Code · Original Test Date · Retake Deadline. One code per test: the same code for the original test and the retake. Put anything unusual about a test in a note under its name.
  4. Fill in Rules for all my tests and For the Testing Center at the bottom.
  5. Share › add testingcenter@d204.lths.net as Viewer. Keep General access Restricted: never “Anyone with the link”, never students.

1. Emails

Choose how Testing Center emails reach you: each one as it happens, one summary after school, or none. Requests for missing test info always come right away.

2. My access-code doc

Paste your doc’s link. It’s used for all your classes and kept from year to year. Only a Google Doc or Google Sheet works.

3. Rules for all my tests

Tick what you allow on every test (your own calculator, the Testing Center’s calculator, scratch paper, writing on the test, notes, open book) and anything the Testing Center should always know (“Please monitor on Securly”). One test can still be different: its testing form has Change for this test.

4. Paper or online?

For each class, what your tests usually are, so the Testing Center can get ready before you fill anything in.

5. If your class is a testing location

B.A.S.E., A.R.C., PSD Resource and Skills for Success classes are testing locations: your own students can test in your room during your class, and teachers can send you their test info and codes. Check the list; press Not my room if one is wrong. You’ll run them from My testing room.

6. Case managers and support-class teachers

Your caseload (from Infinite Campus flags) and the students in your support classes are on My students’ testing: what’s coming up, what happened, who keeps missing, and tests waiting for them. Nothing to set.

During the year

  • Asking a student to take a test: ＋ New test request on My classes. Pick the student, the class and the test, then Testing Center (they’re invited to book a time), their support class, RIR (any student), or a place they’re listed for (Tapestry). No appointment time needed.
  • When a student books one of your tests: you only hear from the app if something is missing (and then once, with a link to fill it in).
  • Change anything: My classes › 🗓️ Set up my year opens these steps again.

Coordinators: the start of the year

  1. Student list: upload the new Infinite Campus export (see Upload a new student list). Include the counselor and flag columns if you can.
  2. Testing locations: Settings › Testing locations › Find classes. Fill in who runs RIR (emails are fine) and add places like Tapestry with their students.
  3. Counselors: Settings › Reports › Counselors (only if the export doesn’t have them).
  4. Seats, periods, bell schedules and no-school days: Settings › Seats & periods, and the sheet’s Bell Schedules and Calendar tabs.
  5. Emails: Switches › Send emails stays Off until you’re ready to go live.

Second semester: on January 1 the app switches to second-semester classes by itself. Nothing to do.
