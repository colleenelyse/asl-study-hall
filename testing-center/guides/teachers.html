The Testing Center for teachers

Sign in with your school Google account and the app opens My classes: your students’ upcoming Testing Center appointments, so you can send the test info once instead of chasing emails.

1. Check your list

Each test has a tracker: Booked → Testing Center has the test → Arrived → Finished. The test step is marked:

  • Ready: the center has what it needs.
  • Check: take a quick look.
  • Needs info: please add testing info.

2. Add testing info

  1. Tap Add testing info to open the testing form.
  2. Fill in: online or paper, platform, access code, time limit, extended time, calculators, scratch paper, open book / notes, and notes.
  3. You fill it in once per test. Every student taking that test gets it.

3. Save time

  • Standing resources: attach something you reuse (for example, a live Google Doc of access codes) to one course or all classes. Students never see these.
  • My usual formats: set the usual format for each course.

4. Emails you get

  • When your student books, finishes, is a no-show, or cancels.
  • A missing-materials reminder the school day before.
  • The email link opens that exact appointment.

5. Choose your emails

The first time you open the app it asks; change it later in My settings:

  • emails as they happen,
  • one summary after school, or
  • none.

6. See what happened

  • Notes from the Testing Center (late, finished early, anything unusual) show on your page and in your emails.
  • Student view lets you look at one of your own students’ pages. It’s look only.

You only see your own students. Access codes and resources go straight to Testing Center staff, never to students.
