The Testing Center app for administrators

One school-owned app for booking and running the North and South Campus Testing Centers. It’s a Google Sheet plus an Apps Script web app in a school Google account. Everyone signs in with their school Google account and sees the view meant for them: students, teachers, or Testing Center staff.

What it replaces

  • SignUpGenius for booking
  • Paper make-up forms
  • Email chains for access codes and test details

Cost: free. It runs on the Google tools LTHS already has.

Where the data lives

  • One Google Sheet in a school Google account.
  • Shared only with Testing Center staff.
  • No outside services.
  • A weekly backup copy and a daily health check.

Who sees what

  • School sign-in, checked on every request.
  • Students see only their own records.
  • Teachers see only their own students.
  • Staff use a hub password.
  • Everything sensitive is logged.

Schedules from Infinite Campus

  1. The Infinite Campus schedule export goes into the IC_Raw_Import tab.
  2. The app shows a preview before anything changes.
  3. It’s all-or-nothing, with a backup made first.

Pilot plan

  1. Staff first: Testing Center staff run the day in the Coordinator hub while students keep booking in SignUpGenius (those bookings are imported).
  2. Then students: students switch to booking in the app, and SignUpGenius is retired.

See it for yourself: the demo with made-up students is at https://asl.cool/testing-center/. One-page guides for students, teachers and coordinators are ready to share.
