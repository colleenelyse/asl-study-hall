Your access-code doc

One Google Doc where you keep the access codes for your online tests. You share it with the Testing Center once and add it to the Testing Center app once. After that, whenever one of your students comes to test, the Testing Center opens your doc and finds the code. No more emailing codes for each student.

What it is

  • A Google Doc with a table: class, test, access code, where the test is (Canvas, lockdown browser, AP Classroom…), how long it's open, and notes.
  • It's yours. You edit it whenever a code changes; the Testing Center always sees the newest version.
  • Students never see it. In the app it shows only to Testing Center staff.

1. Make your copy

  1. Open the template link (in the app: My classes › Standing resources › Make my copy of the template).
  2. Click Make a copy.
  3. Name it Access Codes – your last name, for example Access Codes – Gibbons. Everyone uses this pattern so the Testing Center can find each doc in its Drive.
  4. Fill in a row for each test that has a code. Delete the example row.

2. Share it with the Testing Center

  1. In your doc, click Share (top right).
  2. Type testingcenter@d204.lths.net.
  3. Choose Viewer, uncheck Notify people if you like, and click Share.
  4. Leave General access as Restricted. Never share it with students or post it in Canvas.

3. Add it to the Testing Center app (once)

  1. Copy the doc's address from the address bar.
  2. In the app: My classes › Standing resources › + Add.
  3. Type: Live Google Doc with access codes. Name: the same as your doc, Access Codes – Gibbons (the app fills it in). Paste the link.
  4. Pick All my classes (or only the classes it covers) and click Save.

How it works after that

  • When your student books, the Testing Center sees your doc on that appointment under Access the test, with an Open button.
  • The test shows Check (amber) instead of Needs info (red) on your tracker: the code is probably in your doc. Fill in the testing form if anything else is special (time limit, calculator, notes).
  • Change a code? Just edit your doc. Nothing to re-add or resend. Starting a new school year? Keep the same doc and update it, or make a fresh copy and swap the link in Standing resources.

Naming: always Access Codes – Last name (one doc per teacher, all your classes in it). Already keep codes somewhere else? Any Google Doc or Sheet works the same way: rename it to the pattern, share it with testingcenter@d204.lths.net as Viewer, and add its link as a standing resource.
