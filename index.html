🤟 ASL Study Hall

Welcome! ASL Study Hall is a free place to practice American Sign Language and learn about Deaf culture and hearing. It is made by an ASL teacher for her students and for learners everywhere.

What you can do here

Practice reading fingerspelling from a realistic hand at real speeds, build vocabulary with games and puzzles, and explore how hearing, hearing loss and cochlear implants work. Everything is free, and nothing you do here is saved on our end.

Students: each activity has its own address, like asl.cool/ followed by a word. Ask your teacher for today's address, then type it in the address bar at the top of the screen, or tap the link or QR code your teacher shares.
