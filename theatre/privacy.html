Theatre app: privacy

For the class theatre app at asl.cool/theatre. Last updated October 24, 2026.

This app helps a high school theatre class put on a musical: Company members practice their lines and songs, and families get news, forms and reminders. It is run by the theatre teacher. It has no ads, and nothing in it is sold or shared.

Signing in with Google (parents and families)

Parents sign in with a Google account so the teacher knows who they are. Google tells the app only your name and email address. The app uses them for two things: to know which family you are, and to send you the emails you choose (news, changes for your Company member, copies of their emails).

Our server checks Google’s sign-in and then forgets it: it does not keep your email. Your name and email are kept only in the teacher’s locked list, which only the teacher’s password opens.

What families send

Forms in the app (permission to help with trying on clothes, clothing sizes, sensory and clothing needs, the show-week agreement, permission to film rehearsals for learning) are locked on your phone before they are sent, and only the teacher can open them.

Company members

Everything about the cast (names, roles, practice records, photos and recordings) is stored locked with the class password. Anyone without it sees only scrambled data.

The app counts which pages get used, with no names and nothing that tells who is who, so the teacher can improve it.

Emails

Emails come from the teacher’s school email account. Every email has a Stop these emails link. You can also change what you get on the app’s Email updates page.

Removing your information

Ask the theatre teacher, and your information will be removed. On a phone or iPad, Settings › Lock this iPad removes the app’s copy and your sign-in from that device.

Questions: ask the theatre teacher.
