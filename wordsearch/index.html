Advanced Puzzle Safety
WordSearch Studio already prevents accidental prohibited strings using its master safety list. Use this only when you want one particular puzzle to avoid an extra letter sequence—for example, a student's name or another word you do not want to appear accidentally in the filler letters. This does not remove intentionally entered puzzle words. For permanent changes to the master safety list, edit the Blocked_Words tab in the original Google Sheet.
