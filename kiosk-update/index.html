Update Your Kiosk

Get the newest Hall Pass Kiosk in your own Sheet. You keep your link, students, photos, settings and history. About 5 minutes.

Which release do you have?

Look at the top of your teacher view, next to "Updated … ago". It says Release 1 · check for updates, for example. That link opens this page already set.

Update in 5 steps

  1. Open your code. Open your kiosk Sheet, then Extensions › Apps Script. Your files are listed on the left (Code.gs, Index.html, …).
  2. Paste each file below. For each one: click Copy here, then in Apps Script click that file's name on the left, click anywhere in the code, press Ctrl+A (⌘+A on a Mac) to select it all, and Ctrl+V (⌘+V) to paste over it. Tick the box here when it's done.

    A file isn't in your list? Click + next to Files, choose HTML, type its name without ".html" (for example Tools), press Enter, then paste.

    appsscript.json is hidden at first: click the ⚙️ Project Settings gear on the far left, tick Show "appsscript.json" manifest file in editor, then go back to ‹› Editor and you'll see it.

  3. Save. Press Ctrl+S (⌘+S), or click the 💾 save icon above the code.
  4. Turn on the new parts. In the menu next to ▷ Run, choose setUpMyKiosk and click ▷ Run. If Google asks for permission again, allow it (the same way as when you set it up).
  5. Publish the new version. Click Deploy › Manage deployments, click the ✏️ pencil, open Version and choose New version, then click Deploy. Your kiosk link stays exactly the same.

Then reload the kiosk and your teacher view. The teacher view shows the new release number.
