Grade Calculator

For any class on the 90 / 10 system: Assessments count 90%, Coursework 10%.

Paste or type your numbers from Infinite Campus. Then try a score (What if I get…?) or work backwards (What do I need?). Paste again every week or two to see how you’re doing.

📋 Paste from Infinite Campus

In Infinite Campus Grades, click a class so every assignment is listed, press Ctrl + A, Ctrl + C, and paste it here with Ctrl + V. Then do your next class. Each one goes with its own class.

4🔁 I’m doing a retake

At LTHS a retake replaces your old score on a summative (Assessments, 90%). The old score is thrown out and the test keeps the same points possible.

📋 To use this, paste your whole class page from Infinite Campus into the paste box at the top. Typing your numbers in step 1 isn’t enough: the retake calculator needs each test’s score, and those only come from the pasted page.

An estimate from your numbers, using the 90% / 10% weights and nothing else changing. Letter grades use A 89.5, B 79.5, C 69.5, D 59.5. Infinite Campus is your official grade.
